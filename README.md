# Sachin Timalsena - Personal Portfolio Website

A clean, responsive, and modern personal website built with **HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts (Source Serif 4 & Work Sans), and Material Symbols Outlined**.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Clean Design Ratio & Palette**: Exact design consistency based on Google Stitch tokens with Crimson/Red accents (`#970000`), slate secondary tones, and crisp card borders.
2. **Hero & Profile Showcase**: Highlights your status as an **ACCA Candidate** & **TU BBS Student** with quick-action contact badges and your profile photo.
3. **Structured Core Expertise & Skills**: Hard skills bento cards (Financial Modeling, Data Analysis, Financial Accounting, MS Office & AI) and interactive soft skill chips.
4. **Education & Experience Timeline**: Clear breakdown of:
   - **ACCA** (Running, Knowledge level)
   - **TU BBS** (Running)
   - **Plus Two Science (Bio)** (Liverpool International College)
   - **SEE** (Minduka Secondary School)
   - **AI Upskilling & Tech Integration**
   - **Forward-thinking Team Leader**
5. **Interactive Contact & Socials**:
   - Quick one-click **Copy to Clipboard** for email & phone number.
   - Interactive Email Message Form generating direct `mailto:` links.
   - Social links to LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.
6. **Dark / Light Mode Toggle**: Seamless theme switching with saved preference in `localStorage`.
7. **Mobile-First Responsive Layout**: Smooth scrolling, mobile drawer menu, and active navigation indicator.
